ABOUT

A focused prospecting product.

FreshTradeLeads transforms official California contractor licensing records into focused, current lead reports for insurance, commercial brokerage, and surety professionals. It is an independent service—not a government agency and not affiliated with or endorsed by CSLB.

The service is deliberately simple: buy one clearly defined report, download it immediately, and use it without an account or subscription.
