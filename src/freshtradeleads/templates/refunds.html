Because reports are digital products delivered immediately, purchases are generally final once download access is provided. Replace this example address before deployment.

Approved refunds are returned through Stripe to the original payment method. Full refunds revoke report-download access; partial refunds do not change the original report contents or download period. The time for a refund to appear is determined by Stripe, the card network, and the buyer's financial institution.
